THE PROBLEM

Inspection data lives in folders. It shouldn't.

Every audit, every containment event, every engineering review starts the same way — digging through dated folders across network drives to reconstruct what happened.

01

Retrieval takes hours or days.

Finding every inspection on one component requires navigating thousands of folders organized by date, job number, or inspector — not by the thing being inspected.

02

Traceability lives in tribal knowledge.

The person who remembers where the 2018 PT scans are stored is the only person who can find them. When they leave, that knowledge leaves with them.

03

Audits become scavenger hunts.

Proving compliance means pulling records across disconnected systems, manually verifying that every required inspection was performed, recorded, and archived correctly.

THE APPROACH

Asset-centric by design.

Nexus inverts the model. Your data is organized around the physical things you inspect — not the dates, jobs, or inspectors who touched them.

01 / STRUCTURE
Every file is linked to a component. Every component rolls up to a system. Every system belongs to a site.
Hierarchical model
02 / GOVERNANCE
Upload through a wizard that captures procedure, asset, result, and file roles at the point of creation.
Metadata at source
03 / TRACEABILITY
SHA-256 hashes on every file. Immutable audit trail on every action. Proof of what existed, when, and whether it changed.
Cryptographic verification
04 / RETRIEVAL
Click a component. See every inspection ever performed on it. Open the evidence files in one click.
Seconds, not hours
